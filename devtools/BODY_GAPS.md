# Human Body 360: gaps against the leading anatomy apps and NCERT 6–10

Checked 16 Nov 2026 against the store listings and feature pages of Virtual Medicine **Human Anatomy VR** (Quest:
15 body systems, 13,000+ structures, microanatomy models, 500+ movement animations, "ant mode", quizzes, high-school
and university levels, 15 languages, works offline), **Complete Anatomy** (17,000+ structures, real-time muscle motion
with origin/insertion mapping, beating heart, 39+ microanatomy models, cross-sections and virtual dissection, nerve and
blood-supply tracers, radiology, courses, custom quizzes, multi-user AR), **BioDigital Human** (8,000+ models, 600+
conditions, guided tours, quizzes, embeddable) and **Visible Body** (Atlas, physiology animations, Muscles & Kinesiology
with muscle actions, quizzes, AR). NCERT topics: Class 6 body movements; Class 7 nutrition, respiration, transport and
excretion; Class 8 adolescence and hormones; Class 9 cells and tissues; Class 10 life processes, control and coordination,
human eye.

## Where we already lead for Indian schools
- English, spoken-classroom Hindi and Hinglish everywhere, with a recorded teacher voice; works offline on phones,
  phone VR boxes and Quest. None of the four apps has Hindi narration.
- Guided process journeys with moving particles (oxygen, food, blood, urine, nerve signals) that follow NCERT order.
- Organ Labs with living animation (beating heart with ECG, breathing lungs, focusing eye, vibrating ear bones).
