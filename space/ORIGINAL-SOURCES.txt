# Original Space Imagery and 3D Sources for the Solar System Site and Meta Quest VR

Checked on 15 Oct 2026. Each entry is tagged with how far it was checked:

- **[V]** Verified. The page was opened, and the formats, sizes and licence were read from it, or the files were listed through the site's API.
- **[P]** Partly verified. The page is live, but some details (resolution, terms) could not be confirmed.
- **[U]** Not verified. The site could not be reached from here, or the claim rests on secondary sources.

These sources are not mirrors or fan re-uploads. Each one is the agency's own archive, or a named author's original work built from mission data.

## 1. 3D models (planets, moons, spacecraft)

### 1.1 NASA 3D Resources [V]
- **URL:** https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/ (the old nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov now redirects here)
- **Offers:** A catalogue of mission models, 3D-print files and textures. The Planet, Lunar, Mars, Satellite and Rocket collections cover asteroids, Apollo landing sites, spacecraft and rockets.
- **Formats:** The site's format filter lists 3ds, blender, fbx, glb, jpg, lwo, max, maya, openvsp, stl and tif.
- **Licence:** The page says "All of these assets are free to download and use", subject to the NASA Images & Media Usage Guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
- **Use:** Search a mission, take the GLB, and load it with three.js `GLTFLoader`.

### 1.2 NASA VTAD planet, moon and spacecraft models (GLB + USDZ) [V]
These are individual NASA Science pages. Each has a GLB (for web and WebXR) and a USDZ (for iOS AR). The credit line is "NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD)". All the URLs below returned HTTP 200 with GLB/USDZ links on 15 Oct 2026.

- **Planets:** `https://science.nasa.gov/resource/{mercury|venus|jupiter|saturn|uranus|neptune|pluto}-3d-model/`
  - Mars is at https://science.nasa.gov/resource/planet-mars-3d-model/
  - Example sizes: Jupiter GLB 11.5 MB, Saturn GLB 5.5 MB, Uranus GLB 0.17 MB
- **Earth and Moon:** No landing page was found. The direct GLB assets do exist:
  - https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/solar/2023/09/e/Earth_1_12756.glb
  - https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/solar/2023/09/m/Moon_1_3474.glb
  - For a better Moon, see 1.4.
- **Moons and small bodies:** `https://science.nasa.gov/resource/{europa|io|ganymede|callisto|titan|enceladus|charon|ceres|vesta|bennu}-3d-model/`
  - No pages were found for the Sun, Phobos or Deimos.
- **Spacecraft:** `https://science.nasa.gov/resource/{voyager|hubble-space-telescope|cassini|juno|curiosity|new-horizons|parker-solar-probe|europa-clipper|international-space-station|osiris-rex|mars-reconnaissance-orbiter|insight-lander|mars-ingenuity-helicopter}-3d-model/`
  - Perseverance: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mars-perseverance-rover-3d-model/ (GLB 11.15 MB, USDZ 12.7 MB)
  - Example sizes: ISS GLB 44.5 MB, Hubble GLB 11.5 MB, Voyager GLB 3.1 MB
- **JWST:** A 3D-print STL kit (ZIP, ~96 MB) is at https://science.nasa.gov/?p=894835. For a display GLB, use the GitHub repo (1.3).
- **Licence:** NASA guidelines (see section 0).
- **Use:** These are drop-in GLBs. The planet models are textured spheres, so for close-ups swap in the higher-resolution maps from section 2. Compress the ISS model (gltf-transform draco/meshopt) before shipping it to the Quest.

### 1.3 GitHub: nasa/NASA-3D-Resources [V]
- **URL:** https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
- **Offers:** The full mirror of the catalogue.
  - **3D Models:** 227 folders. Among them are the ISS (5 variants, including "High Res" and "Internal"), JWST (A/B), Hubble (A/B), Voyager (A/B), Cassini, Juno, Curiosity, Perseverance (GLB + .blend), Ingenuity, LRO, MAVEN, MRO, Spirit/Opportunity, the Apollo Lunar Module, Saturn V and more.
  - **Images and Textures:** 48 folders, each with a JPG and a TIF. Planets: Earth A/B, Mars, Moon, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn, Neptune, Pluto. Moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Enceladus, Mimas, Rhea, Dione, Tethys, Iapetus, Ariel, Miranda, Oberon, Titania, Umbriel, Triton, Charon, Phobos, Deimos. Star maps: **Hipparcos, Tycho and Yale Bright Star**.
  - **3D Printing:** 106 folders.
- **Formats:** Counted across the repo: 342 STL, 257 GLB, 49 TIF, 13 .blend, 29 .7z archives.
- **Status:** The last commit was 3 Jun 2025 and the repo is not archived.
- **Licence:** The README calls the assets "free and without copyright" and points to the NASA guidelines.
- **Use:** The repo is very large. Use `git clone --filter=blob:none --sparse` and then a `sparse-checkout` of only the folders you need, or download single files from the GitHub web UI.

### 1.4 NASA SVS "Moon 3D Models for Web, AR, and Animation" [V]
- **URL:** https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14959 (released 29 Jan 2026)
- **Offers:** LRO-based Moon models: `moon.glb`, `moon_small.glb`, `Moon_NASA_LRO_8k_Topo(.glb/_Small.glb)`, `Moon_NASA_LRO_15k_Grid(.glb/.usdz)`, and `Moon_NASA_LRO_23k_Topo/Flat.usdz`.
- **Licence:** NASA. Credit "NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio".
- **Use:** This is the best ready-made Moon for WebXR. Use the `_Small` GLB on Quest.

### 1.5 ESO 3D models [P]
- **URL:** https://www.eso.org/public/products/models3d/ (15 items)
- **Offers:** Telescopes and observatories, not planets.
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0.
- **Use:** An optional "observatories of the world" corner.

### 1.6 NASA Trek portals (Moon, Mars and other bodies) [P]
- **URL:** https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/ and https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/ (both live)
- **Offers:** Browser GIS of mission mosaics and elevation. A 2015 NASA release said users can export terrain for 3D printing; that export was not re-checked here.
- **Use:** Cutting high-resolution terrain patches, for example a landing-site diorama.

## 2. Textures, maps and global mosaics (equirectangular)

### 2.1 NASA SVS: CGI Moon Kit [V]
- **URL:** https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
- **Colour maps:**
  - 2025 version: float16 EXR (943 MB), 16-bit sRGB TIFFs at 4K (4096×2048), 8K (8192×4096) and 16K (16384×8192), plus a 2K JPG.
  - 2019 version: 24-bit TIFFs up to 27360×13680.
- **Displacement maps:** TIFFs at 4, 16 and 64 px/deg (1440×720, 5760×2880, 23040×11520).
  - Float TIFF values are in km relative to a 1737.4 km radius.
  - The uint16 version is in half-metres with a +20,000 offset.
- **Licence:** NASA. Credit "NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio". The maps are "optimized for aesthetics, not science".
- **Use:** Use the 4K or 8K colour map plus the 4 ppd displacement map on a three.js `SphereGeometry` (`displacementMap`). Convert to KTX2 or JPG for the Quest.

### 2.2 Solar System Scope textures [V]
- **URL:** https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- **Offers:**
  - Sun, Mercury, Venus (surface and atmosphere), Earth (day, night, clouds, normal, specular), Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn ring (alpha PNG), Uranus and Neptune.
  - **Stars** and **Stars + Milky Way** sky maps.
  - "Fictional" Ceres, Haumea, Makemake and Eris.
- **Formats:** Mostly 2K and 8K JPG (Venus atmosphere and the dwarf planets go up to 4K). Uranus and Neptune are 2K only. The Earth normal and specular maps are TIF.
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. The page says "use, adapt, and share these textures for any purpose, even commercially".
- **Caveat:** These are not raw originals. They are built from NASA data, but unmapped gaps are filled with **fictional terrain** and the colours are deliberately **more saturated**.
- **Use:** The quickest consistent set for the whole system. The 8K stars + Milky Way JPG makes an easy skybox.

### 2.3 USGS Astrogeology: Astropedia global mosaics [V]
- **URL:** https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search
- **Examples:**
  - Mars Viking Colorized Global Mosaic 232 m (256 px/deg, **12 GB** download): https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_colorized_global_mosaic_232m
  - Moon LRO LROC WAC Global Morphology Mosaic 100 m (**5.5 GB**): https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/moon_lro_lroc_wac_global_morphology_mosaic_100m
  - The site also offers collections for Jupiter Voyager/Galileo, Mercury MESSENGER, Venus Magellan and Moon Kaguya TC.
- **Formats:** Mostly GeoTIFF or ISIS cubes, simple-cylindrical (equirectangular) projection.
- **Licence:** The metadata says **"Access Constraints: public domain; Use Constraints: None"**.
- **Use:** The source of truth for science-grade maps. Downsample in GDAL to 8K or 16K, then tile.

### 2.4 Björn Jónsson planetary maps [V]
- **URL:** https://bjj.mmedia.is/data/planetary_maps.html
- **Offers:** Hand-processed true-colour maps from raw Voyager, Galileo and Cassini data in the PDS:
  - Venus
  - Jupiter (Cassini 2000, Voyager 1, and Voyager 2, including a 2026 release)
  - Io (plus a largely fictional DEM), Europa, Ganymede, Callisto
  - Saturn (original 9000×4500; the web copy is 2880×1440)
  - Saturn's rings, Rhea, Neptune
- **Terms:** The maps are free to use without permission if you credit "created by Björn Jónsson". However, he asks people **not to place copies on their website**, because he updates them.
- **Use:** These are the most faithful Jupiter and Saturn colours. **Email him for permission** before hosting them as WebXR textures, or use them only in rendered videos or images.

### 2.5 NASA Blue Marble (Earth) [P]
- **URL:** https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/collections/blue-marble/ (the old Visible Earth URL redirects here)
- **Offers:** NASA's monthly true-colour Earth mosaics, at up to 86400×43200 historically.
- **Licence:** NASA.
- **Use:** The Earth day map. Pair it with the Solar System Scope night, cloud and specular maps.

### 2.6 Planet Pixel Emporium [U]
- **URL:** https://planetpixelemporium.com/planets.html
- **Status:** The site was unreachable from here (DNS and proxy errors).
- **Terms:** Secondary sources only say "mostly free… read the copyright info". The terms could not be confirmed, so treat it as **non-commercial unless purchased**.
- **Use:** Prefer 2.1–2.5 instead.

## 3. 360° photos (panoramas)

### 3.1 Mars: Perseverance Mastcam-Z 360° Panorama Collection [V]
- **URL:** https://mastcamz.asu.edu/mastcam-zs-360-panorama-collection/ (updated 18 Sep 2026)
- **Offers:** **56** full-azimuth panoramas from sols 0 to 1968, as JPEG, PNG and TIFF at full camera resolution. Some files are several GB.
- **Projection:** Simple cylindrical, with north in the centre. Elevation typically runs **−70° to +10°**, so these are **partial, not full 360×180**. Only a subset covers all the ground out to the horizon.
- **Terms:** "Available for public or scientific community use without the need for explicit permission." Credit "NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS" and, if possible, cite their LPSC abstract.
- **Use:** Map onto a sphere with a computed `thetaStart` and `thetaLength`, and fill the zenith with a sky gradient.

### 3.2 Mars: individual Perseverance 360° panoramas on NASA Science [V]
- **First 360 (Sol 3):** https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mastcam-zs-first-360-degree-panorama/
  - JPEG, 51.8 MB, about 36952×11570.
- **Van Zyl Overlook:** https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mastcam-zs-360-degree-view-of-van-zyl-overlook
  - PIA24663 PNG, 23265×5165, 149 MB (from a 2.4-gigapixel original).
  - Rover parts were patched, and the sky was "digitally smoothed and expanded".
- **Credit:** NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS. Both are partial vertical coverage.

### 3.3 Mars: DLR Jezero interactive map with panoramic viewpoints [V]
- **URL:** https://maps.planet.fu-berlin.de/jezero/
- **Offers:** A browser map with clickable panoramic views. ASU says it works in the Quest browser.
- **Note:** The older Quest stereo viewer at `mars-2020-stereo.glitch.me` is **dead** (HTTP 410).
- **Use:** A reference, or a link-out.

### 3.4 Moon: Apollo landing-site 360° panoramas (USGS) [V]
- **URL:** https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/moon-apollo-landing-panoramas
- **Offers:** Panoramas for Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17, built by a USGS team from the astronauts' Hasselblad frames. Example: Apollo 17 Station 6 (`AP17_6.jpg`).
- **Coverage:** **Horizontal strips (360° wide, narrow vertical band)**, not full spheres.
- **Resolution:** The downloads are small (Station 6 is about 0.5 MB).
- **Licence:** A US Government work. The page doesn't state a licence; public domain is expected.
- **Use:** Wrap onto a cylinder inside a starfield sphere with a grey ground plane.

### 3.5 Moon: Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (ALSJ) [P]
- **URL:** https://apollojournals.org/alsj/main.html. The image libraries are at https://apollojournals.org/alsj/picture.html (Apollo 9–17).
- **Offers:** All the surface photos, including high-resolution scans, plus the journal's own assembled pans. Some of those pans are still at nasa.gov/history/alsj/…, for example https://www.nasa.gov/history/alsj/a15/a15pan1480225.html.
- **Terms:** The photos are NASA. The site itself is "© Eric M. Jones". Volunteer-assembled pans may carry the assembler's credit, so check each one.
- **Not found:** A single "panoramas index" page on the new domain.

### 3.6 Moon: NASA JSC "Panoramas of Apollo sites" [V]
- **Where:** NASA Image Library (images.nasa.gov), search "Panoramas of Apollo sites". Examples: `jsc2007e045375`, `jsc2007e045377`, `jsc2008e040725`.
- **Offers:** JSC-assembled panoramas from Apollo 11 and 14 frames. These are partial panoramas.

### 3.7 Earth and sky: ESO 360° panoramas [V]
- **URL:** https://www.eso.org/public/images/archive/category/360pano/ (**459** items)
- **True 360×180 example:** "Rare 360-degree panorama of the southern sky", https://www.eso.org/public/images/vlt-mw-potw-cc-extended/
  - 15204×7602, **FOV 360°×180°**.
  - TIFF 101.7 MB; 10K and 4K TIFF and JPEG versions.
  - Credit ESO/H.H. Heyer.
- **Partial example:** "360 degree Paranal panorama", https://www.eso.org/public/images/pano360-paranal-2/
  - 16733×4634, **FOV 360°×99.7°**. Credit ESO/S. Brunier.
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0.
- **Use:** Real night-sky domes from the Atacama. Check the "Field of View" line on each page to pick the 360×180 ones.

### 3.8 Milky Way: ESO "The Milky Way panorama" (GigaGalaxy Zoom) [V]
- **URL:** https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
- **Offers:** The whole sky with the galactic plane horizontal, at a 2:1 aspect.
- **Projection:** The page doesn't name it; the thumbnail looks equirectangular in galactic coordinates.
- **Resolution:** 6000×3000. The 800-megapixel original is not public (copyright S. Brunier).
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. Credit ESO/S. Brunier.
- **Use:** A photographic Milky Way skybox. To align it with celestial coordinates, rotate it by the galactic-to-equatorial transform.

### 3.9 ISS interior: ESA "International Space Station panoramic tour" [V]
- **URL:** https://www.esa.int/Our_Activities/Human_Spaceflight/International_Space_Station/Highlights/International_Space_Station_panoramic_tour
- **Offers:** An interactive 360° of each module (Cupola/Node-3, Zvezda, Zarya, Unity, Destiny and more), stitched from astronaut photos taken in June 2015.
- **Licence:** ESA standard terms (educational use OK with ©ESA; **commercial use needs permission**).
- **Use:** Link-out. Ask ESA before re-hosting the stitched panoramas.

### 3.10 Earth from the ISS: NASA "Gateway to Astronaut Photography of Earth" [V]
- **URL:** https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/
- **Offers:** Every crew Earth-observation photo, searchable, with the latest ISS imagery. These are **not 360°**.
- **Licence:** NASA.
- **Use:** "Earth from the ISS" galleries and window views.

## 6. Star, galaxy and exoplanet data

### 6.1 NASA SVS Deep Star Maps 2020 [V]
- **URL:** https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851
- **Offers:** Star maps built from **about 1.7 billion stars** (Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2 and Gaia DR2), with separate Milky Way and bright-star layers. Also included: constellation figures and the IAU boundaries, and a celestial grid.
- **Formats:** Half-float **OpenEXR**, plate carrée (equirectangular), in **celestial (ICRF/J2000)** or **galactic** coordinates. Sizes: 4K 4096×2048, 8K, 16K, 32K and 64K 65536×32768. The grid and figures are grayscale TIFF.
- **Licence:** NASA. Credit "NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio" plus "Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC". The constellation figures are based on Sky & Telescope (A. MacRobert) and are not official.
- **Use:** **The best real sky background.** Tone-map the 8K EXR to JPG or KTX2. Because it is in celestial coordinates, the RA/Dec of real stars line up when it is mapped inside a sphere.

### 6.2 NASA SVS Deep Star Maps (2012) [V]
- **URL:** https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3895
- **Offers:** About 100 million stars (BSC, Tycho-2, UCAC3).
- **Formats:** **JPG/TIF** at 4K, 8K and 16K in celestial and galactic coordinates, plus constellation overlays.
- **Use:** An easier format for the web than EXR.

### 6.3 ESA Gaia all-sky maps (equirectangular) [V]
- **DR2 "Gaia's sky in colour", equirectangular:** https://sci.esa.int/web/gaia/-/60196-gaia-s-sky-in-colour-equirectangular-projection
  - PNG at 1000×500, 2000×1000, 4000×2000, and **40000×20000 (890 MB)**.
  - The page also links a 360° JPG (8192×4096), but that link returned **404** on 15 Oct 2026.
- **EDR3 "Colour of the sky", equirectangular:** https://sci.esa.int/web/gaia/-/the-colour-of-the-sky-from-gaia-s-early-data-release-3-equirectangular-projection
  - PNG at 2000×1000, 4000×2000, and **16000×8000 (236 MB)**.
- **360° video:** https://sci.esa.int/web/gaia/-/60219-360-view-of-gaia-s-sky (MP4 4096×2048, 172.6 MB)
- **Licence:** **CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO**. Credit "ESA/Gaia/DPAC; CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. Acknowledgement: A. Moitinho" (EDR3).
- **Use:** An "our galaxy as Gaia sees it" sphere, in galactic coordinates (Milky Way along the equator).

### 6.4 Gaia archive (raw catalogue) [V]
- **URL:** https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/
- **Licence:** **CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO** (https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia-users/license). That is non-commercial; commercial use goes through ESA's archive terms.
- **Use:** Avoid it for a commercial product. Use HYG or the SVS maps instead.

### 6.5 HYG Database v4.2 [V]
- **URL:** https://www.astronexus.com/projects/hyg (mirror: https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg)
- **Offers:** All stars from Hipparcos, Yale BSC5 and Gliese, about **120,000 stars**, in a 14 MB CSV. Fields include proper names, Bayer/Flamsteed IDs, RA/Dec, distance, magnitude, colour index and constellation.
- **Licence:** **CC BY-SA 4.0** (older v3.7 is CC BY-SA 2.5).
- **Use:** A real 3D star field: place stars at their actual x/y/z (in parsecs), colour them by B–V, and label the named stars. A related set, AT-HYG (Tycho-2 + Gaia distances, https://www.astronexus.com/projects/at-hyg), was not checked in detail **[P]**.

### 6.6 Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition [P]
- **URL:** https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/V/50 (CDS/VizieR V/50)
- **Offers:** The roughly 9,110 naked-eye stars.
- **Status:** The page is live, but its JavaScript-rendered licence field could not be read. It is also included inside HYG, and the NASA repo has a "Yale Bright Star Map" texture (1.3).
- **Use:** A lightweight naked-eye sky (about 9k points).

### 6.7 NASA Exoplanet Archive [V]
- **URL:** https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/
- **Offers:** Confirmed planets: **6,445** on 15 Oct 2026 (`ps` table, `default_flag=1`). It also has host stars, Kepler/TESS data and more.
- **API:** The TAP endpoint was tested and works:
  `https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/TAP/sync?query=select+pl_name,hostname,sy_dist,pl_rade,ra,dec+from+pscomppars&format=csv`
- **Licence:** NASA public data. It requests its standard acknowledgement text in publications.
- **Use:** Plot exoplanet hosts in the 3D star field and link each one to NASA Eyes on Exoplanets.

### 6.8 Gaia Sky (ESA/ARI open-source app) [V]
- **URL:** https://gaiasky.space/ (v3.8.0, released 23 Jul 2026, licence MPL 2.0)
- **Offers:** A real-time desktop universe built on Gaia data, with **OpenXR VR** support. Its **360 mode renders equirectangular, cylindrical or Hammer frames**.
- **Use:** Render your own 360° fly-through videos of the real star field. Quest needs Link or Air Link to a PC; it is not standalone. Check the data licence (Gaia NC) before commercial release.

## 9. Honest notes

1. **There is no real photo of the Milky Way from outside.** Every face-on or "top-down" Milky Way image is an artist's concept, e.g. NASA/JPL-Caltech/R. Hurt, or ESO's https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso1339g/ (5600×5600, credit NASA/JPL-Caltech/ESO/R. Hurt). Chandra's FAQ puts it this way: "we can only show an artist's representation of what our Galaxy looks like from the outside" (https://chandra.cfa.harvard.edu/resources/faq/sources/milky_way/milky_way-1.html). The real data is the edge-on view from inside: the SVS Deep Star Maps, Gaia maps and ESO eso0932a. Label any top-down galaxy in the site as "Artist's concept".
2. **True 360×180 (full sphere, 2:1 equirect), safe as VR skyboxes:**
   - SVS Deep Star Maps 2012/2020 and the Gaia equirectangular maps
   - ESO `vlt-mw-potw-cc-extended` (360°×180°) and ESO `eso0932a` (whole sky)
   - Solar System Scope stars and Milky Way textures
   - The SVS 360 videos (black hole, COBE, Bennu, JWST/Hubble tours) and the NASA JPL/ESA YouTube 360 videos. In the rover 360 videos, NASA fills or smooths gaps; e.g. the Van Zyl sky was "digitally smoothed and expanded".
3. **Partial panoramas, not full spheres. Fill the sky and ground yourself:**
   - The Mastcam-Z panoramas (360° azimuth, about −70° to +10° elevation)
   - ESO `pano360-paranal-2` (360°×99.7°)
   - The Apollo panoramas (360° horizontal strips)
   - The ESO ALMA/VLT VR master (cylindrical)
   - Fulldome masters (180° fisheye domemaster)
4. **Planet "textures" are maps, not 360 photos.** They are equirectangular surface maps to wrap onto spheres. The Solar System Scope maps include fictional gap-fill and boosted saturation. Use the USGS or SVS maps where accuracy matters.
5. **"Official" doesn't always mean free for commercial use.** Check section 0, especially ESA main-site media, the Gaia archive data (NC) and Björn Jónsson's no-rehost request.
6. **Things not verified here:**
   - Planet Pixel Emporium terms (site unreachable)
   - The PRADAN data-use terms (behind a login)
   - A fulldome category on esawebb.org (404)
   - The Gaia 8K 360 JPG link (404)
   - The pixel sizes of the NASA library 8K Curiosity MP4s (the CDN was blocked from this sandbox)
   - The NASA Trek terrain-export feature
