Privacy Policy — DUET Solar System 360

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Publisher: DUET STEM, a unit of Duet Power Private Limited, India

DUET Solar System 360 lets school students explore the Sun, planets, moons, stars and real spacecraft in 3D, virtual reality and mixed reality, on Meta Quest headsets, on Android phones and tablets, and in web browsers. This policy explains what information the app uses.

What we collect

The app has no sign-up or login, and it does not ask students for personal information: no names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings or location. It uses no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not include any third-party data-collecting software. The only information that reaches us is the small device-register entry described under “Subscription codes”, sent once when a school activates its code; it identifies a device and a school, never a student.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as the chosen view, speed and sound on or off.
  • The subscription activation: a scrambled fingerprint of the school's subscription code, its end date and the school's name.
  • Downloaded pictures, 3D models, sounds and videos, kept so that the app also works offline.

This information is kept on the device; apart from the device-register entry described below, it is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Subscription codes

Schools receive a subscription code from DUET STEM. To check a code, the app downloads a list of scrambled code fingerprints from our website and compares them on the device; the code itself is never sent to us or to anyone else.

A code is sold for a set number of devices. When a code is activated, the app tells the DUET STEM device register (a small service we run on Google Apps Script) the scrambled fingerprint of the code, a random device number created by the app, the kind of device (for example "Meta Quest") and the school's name, so that each code is used on no more devices than were bought. We use this only to count the devices on each code, and keep it while the code is valid. We do not sell or share it. A school can ask us to remove one device or all of its entries at any time by writing to info@duetpower.in.

Camera

On phones and tablets, the Camera AR view uses the camera only after the student taps it, to show the 3D model in the room. The picture stays on the device and is not recorded or sent.

Internet connection

The app downloads its pictures, 3D models, sounds and videos from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as the device's IP address in its server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use this information to identify anyone.

Virtual reality

In VR, the headset's position, controller and hand tracking are processed on the device by the headset and its browser so that the lab responds to movement. The app does not record or send this data. In the headset, the app does not use the headset's cameras or record the microphone.

Children

The app is made for use in schools, under the supervision of teachers. It does not ask students for their name, email address or any other personal information, and nothing it sends identifies a student. On Meta Quest headsets it is meant for users of the age Meta allows (10 years and over).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
