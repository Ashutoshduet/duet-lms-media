Privacy Policy — DUET Solar System 360

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Publisher: DUET STEM, a unit of Duet Power Private Limited, India

DUET Solar System 360 lets school students explore the Sun, planets, moons, stars and real spacecraft in 3D, virtual reality and mixed reality, on Meta Quest headsets, on Android phones and tablets, and in web browsers. This policy explains what information the app uses.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up or login. It does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings or location. It uses no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not include any third-party data-collecting software.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as the chosen view, speed and sound on or off.
  • The subscription activation: a scrambled fingerprint of the school's subscription code, its end date and the school's name.
  • Downloaded pictures, 3D models, sounds and videos, kept so that the app also works offline.

This information stays on the device and is never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Subscription codes

Schools receive a subscription code from DUET STEM. To check a code, the app downloads a list of scrambled code fingerprints from our website and compares them on the device. The code itself is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Internet connection

The app downloads its pictures, 3D models, sounds and videos from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as the device's IP address in its server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use this information to identify anyone.

Virtual reality

In VR, the headset's position, controller and hand tracking are processed on the device by the headset and its browser so that the lab responds to movement. The app does not record or send this data. The app does not use the microphone or the cameras.

Children

The app is made for use in schools, under the supervision of teachers. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
