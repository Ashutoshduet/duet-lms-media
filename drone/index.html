    Controls

    How to fly

    Left stick = throttle and yaw, right stick = pitch and roll (Mode 2, the common setting in India). Switch to Mode 1 in Settings. Arm the motors first, then add throttle.

    RC transmitter (most realistic)

    Connect a Radiomaster, FrSky, Jumper or similar radio by USB and choose USB Joystick (HID) on the radio. Open Settings → Radio to detect each stick and an arm switch. Throttle then works exactly like on a real drone.

    Meta Quest

    Open the page in the Quest Browser and press VR. Left thumbstick = throttle and yaw, right thumbstick = pitch and roll. A = arm, B = reset, X = view, Y = flight mode. Line-of-sight view (standing on the field) is the most comfortable.
