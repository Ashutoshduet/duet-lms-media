    How to fly

    Left stick = throttle and yaw, right stick = pitch and roll (Mode 2, common in India). Switch to Mode 1 in Settings. The remote drawn at the bottom of the screen shows your sticks and switches exactly like the real one.

    Throttle like a real radio

    On an FPV radio the throttle stick has no spring: it stays where you leave it and the bottom means motors at idle. That is the default here (Settings → Controls → Throttle). On the camera drone the left stick springs back to the middle and the drone holds its height, like a DJI remote.

    Flying far away with the camera

    Switch to the camera view (C) and fly using only the screen, like a real pilot 1 km away: watch the map, the home arrow, the distance (D), height (H) and the signal bars. Hills, buildings and trees between you and the drone weaken the signal. When the video breaks up, turn back or climb. If the link is lost, the camera drone flies home by itself; an FPV quad drops (failsafe).

    PS5 / Xbox controller

    Connect by USB or Bluetooth and press any button. Left stick = throttle and yaw, right stick = pitch and roll. The sticks spring back, so the camera drone flies like a DJI remote; for FPV drones the default is centre = hover (Settings → Controls).

    DJI remote (RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3)

    Works in Chrome or Edge on a Windows or Mac computer, opened from the simulator's own address (ashutoshduet.github.io/duet-lms-media/drone). Switch the remote on with the drone off, connect its bottom USB-C port, press Connect DJI remote and pick DJI USB VCOM For Protocol. Sticks and the gimbal dial work; start or stop the motors with both sticks down and in, like on the real remote. On Windows, if no DJI port appears, install DJI Assistant 2 once. Screen remotes (DJI RC Pro, Smart Controller) do not send stick data over USB.

    RC transmitter (most realistic)

    Connect a Radiomaster, FrSky, Jumper or similar radio by USB and choose USB Joystick (HID) on the radio. Open Settings → Radio to map each stick, the arm switch (SA), the flight-mode switch (SB), the beeper (SC) and turtle mode (SD).

    Meta Quest

    The DUET Drone Simulator app opens straight into the headset. In the Quest Browser press Fly in VR or Fly in AR. VR: you stand in the field behind the pad and look all around. AR (mixed reality): the drone takes off from your floor and flies in your own room; the Tiny Whoop is the best size indoors. The grip button opens the headset menu: VR or AR, map, aircraft, view, flight mode, reset and Exit. Point at a button and pull the trigger.
