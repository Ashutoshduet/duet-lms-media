Robotics Lab 360 · credits and licences

Everything not listed below is made by DUET STEM: the 3D board, breadboard and every part (modelled in code), the board's printed artwork, the textures drawn in code, the sounds, the Arduino C/C++ compiler and the microcontroller, the circuit simulator, and the lessons in English, Hindi and Hinglish.

The board is a generic "UNO R3"-style board of our own design, compatible with the Arduino Uno; no Arduino logo or other brand artwork is used. "Arduino" and "Arduino Uno" are trademarks of Arduino SA and are named only to say what the lessons teach. Chip and sensor part numbers (ATmega328P, 2N2222, LM35, HC-SR04, SG90) are used only to describe the parts.
