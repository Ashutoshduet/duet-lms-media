Privacy Policy — DUET Robotics Lab 360

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Publisher: DUET STEM, a unit of Duet Power Private Limited, India

DUET Robotics Lab 360 is a 3D electronics bench for school students. Students place parts on a breadboard, wire them to an Arduino-compatible board, write code and watch the circuit work, on Meta Quest headsets, Android phones and tablets, computers and classroom panels.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up or login. It does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings or location. It uses no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not include any third-party data-collecting software.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as the chosen language (English, Hindi or Hinglish), sound on or off, the lessons completed and the sketch written in Free build.
  • The subscription activation: a scrambled fingerprint of the school's subscription code, its end date and the school's name.
  • The app's own files, kept so that the app also works offline. The sketches students write run inside the app on the device; they are never uploaded.

This information stays on the device and is never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Subscription codes

Schools receive a subscription code from DUET STEM. To check a code, the app downloads a list of scrambled code fingerprints from our website and compares them on the device; the code itself is never sent to us or to anyone else.

A code is sold for a set number of devices. When a code is activated, the app tells the DUET STEM device register (a small service we run on Google Apps Script) the scrambled fingerprint of the code, a random device number created by the app, the kind of device (for example "Meta Quest") and the school's name, so that each code is used on no more devices than were bought. We use this only to count the devices on each code.

Camera

The camera is used only when a student taps "Scan" to read the QR code on a DUET STEM kit. The picture stays on the device and is not recorded or sent.

Internet connection

The app downloads its 3D models and narration from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as the device's IP address in its server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use this information to identify anyone.

Virtual reality

In VR, the headset's position, controller and hand tracking are processed on the device by the headset and its browser so that the lab responds to movement. The app does not record or send this data. In the headset, the app does not use the headset's cameras or record the microphone.

Children

The app is made for use in schools, under the supervision of teachers. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
