HUMAN BODY 360 · DUET STEM
==========================
A see-through 3D human body for Meta Quest (VR and passthrough AR), laptops and phones.
English and Hindi. Works offline after the first visit.

WHAT IS INSIDE
  Explore            Point at any bone or organ to see its name and job (EN + HI).
                     Layers: skin, skeleton, muscles, heart & blood, breathing, digestion,
                     kidneys, brain & nerves, fine vessels & nerves.
  How we breathe     8 steps: nose -> windpipe -> bronchi -> lungs inflate, diaphragm moves,
                     alveoli close-up (O2 into blood, CO2 out), oxygen to the body, CO2 breathed out.
  From food to energy 10 steps: chewing, swallowing, stomach churning, bile and pancreatic juice,
                     villi absorbing food, liver, mitochondria "burning" glucose (ATP),
                     large intestine taking back water, waste out.
  Blood circulation  7 steps: follow one red blood cell heart -> lungs -> heart -> leg -> back.
  Inside the heart   6 steps: four chambers, valves (lub-dub), coronary arteries.
  Kidneys and urine  6 steps: blood in, kidney cut open, nephron filter, ureters, bladder.
  Brain and nerves   5 steps: brain parts, spinal cord signals, vision, reflex arc.
  Bones and muscles  4 steps.

PUT IT ON THE QUEST
  The page must be opened from an https:// address (WebXR does not work from a file).
  1. Upload this whole folder to any https host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, your website),
     e.g. https://your-domain/body/
  2. In the Quest browser open that address once with internet. It saves itself for offline use.
  3. Press "Enter VR" (or "Enter AR" for passthrough). The body stands in front of you.
  4. Optional: browser menu -> "Install app" puts it in the Library like an app.
     The same Bubblewrap / Horizon Alpha-channel route used for the Space Lab apps also works
     with manifest.webmanifest in this folder.

CONTROLS IN THE HEADSET
  Trigger (or pinch)        pick an organ, or press a button on the panels
  Right thumbstick          left/right turns the body, up/down makes it bigger/smaller
  Grip (hold)               grab the body and move it
  A / X                     next step      B / Y   previous step
  Walk around the body to see it from every side. Big steps (heart, kidney) bring the organ
  close and show a magnified bubble of what happens inside.

ON A LAPTOP OR PHONE
  Drag to turn, scroll / pinch to zoom, click any part. Arrow keys: next / previous step,
  Space: play / pause. Narration uses the device's built-in voice (EN or HI) if it has one.
