HUMAN BODY 360 · DUET STEM
==========================
A see-through 3D human body for Meta Quest (VR and passthrough AR), laptops and phones.
English, Hindi and Hinglish, with a teacher voice in all three. Works offline after the first visit.

WHAT IS INSIDE
  Explore            Point at any of 2,400 pieces (every bone, muscle, organ, vessel, nerve, gland):
                     it glows gold, shows its name (EN + HI) and job, and the teacher explains
                     how it works. "Show alone" isolates it, "Close-up" opens a magnified window
                     (in VR the body itself never moves or changes size).
  Body parts         350 parts grouped by system with search, each with name, job and detail in
                     English, Hindi and Hinglish.
  Organ Lab          16 big, detailed models on a pedestal with guided tours:
                     Heart (cut-away, 4 chambers, blood flow, valves lub-dub, SA/AV node and
                     Purkinje electric spark with ECG, coronary arteries), Eye (light rays,
                     pupil, lens focusing, retina, blind spot, optic nerve), Ear (sound waves,
                     eardrum and ossicles vibrating, cochlea, balance, Eustachian tube), Brain
                     (lobes, hemispheres, motor/sensory strips, brainstem, deep parts, neurons),
                     Lungs (bronchial tree, breathing muscles, alveoli, blood), Kidney (cut open,
                     nephron, urine path, blood in/out), Tooth (enamel, dentine, pulp, roots,
                     cavities), Skin (layers, sweat, touch, goosebumps), Mouth, Intestines, Liver, Knee,
                     Urinary system (both kidneys cut open, nephron, ureters, a bladder that fills
                     and empties), Pancreas and spleen (head/body/tail, pancreatic juice, islets
                     and insulin, blood filter), Arm muscles and reflex (a real arm that bends: the
                     biceps shortens and bulges, the triceps lengthens; muscle fibres; touch a
                     candle flame and watch the reflex arc pull the hand away), Leg muscles and knee
                     jerk (hamstrings bend the knee, quadriceps straightens it through the kneecap;
                     a reflex hammer taps the tendon and the leg kicks).
  Journeys           Breathing (15 steps, oxygen carried to every part of the body), Food to
                     energy (19), Blood circulation (7), Inside the heart (6), Kidneys (6),
                     Brain and nerves (5), Bones and muscles (4), Reflex: touch a hot tawa (6),
                     Hormones: the glands and their chemical messages (7).
  Take apart         Organs or bones float out of the body, labelled; point at any piece.
  Put back game      Drag (or grab in VR) each organ or bone back to its place in the body.
  Hands-on           Take any part out of the body: in VR squeeze the grip on it (or pinch and hold);
                     turn it in your hand, leave it in the air, resize it with both hands, bring it
                     near its place to put it back. On a screen: pick a part, Take out, then drag it.
  Cut                VR: a see-through blade in your hand cuts away everything between your hand and
                     the blade (trigger leaves it in place). Screen: a slice from the front, side or
                     top that moves deeper with a button.
  Teacher voice      Recorded offline narration in English, Hindi and Hinglish; voice and
                     sound effects can each be switched on/off.

PUT IT ON THE QUEST
  The page must be opened from an https:// address (WebXR does not work from a file).
  1. Upload this whole folder to any https host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, your website),
     e.g. https://your-domain/body/
  2. In the Quest browser open that address once with internet. It saves itself for offline use
     (the teacher voice files are saved in the background).
  3. Press "Enter VR" (or "Enter AR" for passthrough). The body stands in front of you.
  4. The DUET Human Body VR app (Trusted Web Activity) opens this address straight into VR.

CONTROLS IN THE HEADSET
  Trigger (or pinch)        pick an organ, or press a button on the panels
  Right thumbstick          left/right turns the body, up/down makes it bigger/smaller
  Grip (hold)               grab the body and move it
  A / X                     next step      B / Y   previous step
  Menu panel                language (EN / हिंदी / Hinglish), Voice on/off, Sounds on/off,
                            size, Recenter (puts the body back in front of you), Exit VR
  The body stays where it is when you look at or press the panels. Walk around it to see it
  from every side.

ON A LAPTOP OR PHONE
  Drag to turn, scroll / pinch to zoom, click any part. Arrow keys: next / previous step,
  Space: play / pause.
